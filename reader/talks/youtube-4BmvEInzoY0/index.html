A Walk-through of Computational Reflection in Coq - Boston Haskell Meetup

Gregory Malecha

November Boston Haskell Meetup @ ThoughBot

Gregory Malecha presents techniques for computational reflection in the Coq proof assistant. Computational reflection is a proof technique that makes use of programs written within the language of the proof assistant itself, rather than a meta-language.
